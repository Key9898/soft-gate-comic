# Impl 118 — 404 recovery to hand-off bar

One shared recovery for unknown URLs, missing series, and missing episodes. Peer is a webtoon keep-going 404, not a mascot page.

## What shipped

- `NotFoundPage` variants `page` / `series` / `episode`. Broken URL stays in the bar (no `/not-found` or homepage redirect).
- `SearchAutocomplete`, guest-safe Go here (Categories / Popular / New / Help / Contact — not Library), demo 3:4 `BookCard` tiles, Help/Contact.
- Detail and Reader thin empty states replaced by the same component.
- SEO: `noindex`, `omitJsonLd`, `omitCanonical`. SPA HTTP 200 until a later host Impl.
