# Impl 147 — Home For You rail

Home For You uses `forYouWebtoons`: Subscribe, likes, history, then same-genre neighbors sorted by `id`. Not Ranking or Trending. Guest and no-signal hide the rail. Continue ids are excluded. Cards go to `/webtoon/:id`.
