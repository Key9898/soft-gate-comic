# Impl 150 — Home Daily weekday board

Home Daily sits after Trending and before Updated. It is a weekday schedule board, not a restyle of Updated.

## Data

- `Webtoon.uploadDay?: 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6` matches `Date.getDay()` (0 = Sunday).
- Demo assignment (ongoing only): Sun Ocean Dreams, Mon The Last Horizon, Tue Love in Seoul, Wed Shadow Knight, Thu Blood Moon, Fri Cyber Dreams, Sat Campus Life.
- Golden Age (completed) and Forest Spirit (hiatus) omit `uploadDay`.
- `SHARED_DATA_SCHEMA_VERSION` **10**.

## Home

- Chips Mon–Sun (`UPLOAD_DAY_ORDER`), default today, `aria-pressed`, `min-h-11`.
- Filter: `status === 'ongoing'` and `uploadDay === day`. Sort `viewCount`, cap 6.
- No View all (same as Trending). Empty day keeps heading + chips + empty copy.
- Honesty: Demo weekday schedule, not latest episode dates, not a live upload clock.
- Same title may also appear on Updated / Ranking / Trending. Do not strip Daily ids from those rails.

## Out of scope

wait-for-free. Categories Daily sort. Live publisher calendar. Fake 23:59 timer. For You / Follow / Search Demo chips.
