# Impl 97 — Mock calendar stays in 2026

## Why

Impl 96 put series `createdAt`/`updatedAt` in 2026. Episodes and mock users still used 2024, so episode lists and admin user rows looked like a three-year-old catalog. Campus Life episodes were dated September — a future month if the year were only swapped.

## What shipped

- Episode dates sit inside each series window (`createdAt` … `updatedAt`), first episode on series start, last episode on series `updatedAt`. None after **2026-08-17**.
- Campus Life compressed into 10–15 Aug 2026 (series window is five days).
- Mock users joined in 2026; David Brown moved to Feb so join ≤ last login.
- Scheduled slot moved to **2026-08-18** (still scheduled, not a stale April date).
- `SHARED_DATA_SCHEMA_VERSION` **6**
